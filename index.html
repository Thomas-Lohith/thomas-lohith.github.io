About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Università di Pavia, conducting industrial doctoral research in collaboration with Milano Serravalle – Milano Tangenziali. My work applies machine learning and deep learning to large-scale sensor networks, drawing on a broadly transferable skill set: building data pipelines, engineering features from complex multivariate data, training and validating models, and deploying them so they run reliably beyond the notebook environment.

Previously, I was a visiting researcher at OWL University of Applied Sciences in Lemgo, Germany, a working student programming collaborative robots, and a materials-testing researcher in India. Across these roles, the common thread has been working with large, real-world datasets and making them trustworthy, analyzable, and useful, whether the work is described as data science, ML engineering, or applied AI research.
